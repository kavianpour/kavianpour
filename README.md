## 🧠 About Me

I'm an **AI Solutions Architect and researcher** with a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world impact. My work spans:

- 🔧 **Fault Diagnosis** — Deep learning & GNNs for rotary machinery under noisy, imbalanced, and missing-data conditions
- 🌍 **Earthquake Prediction** — CNN-BiLSTM models with attention mechanisms for seismic magnitude classification
- 🚢 **Maritime AI** — Ship fuel consumption prediction and route/speed optimization aligned with IMO emission policies
- 🐄 **Smart Agriculture** — IoT-based calving time prediction with real-time health monitoring for livestock
- 🤖 **LLMs & Conversational AI** — RAG pipelines, fine-tuning (LLaMA, FLAN-T5), and production chatbot deployment

> *Turning complex research into impactful technologies — one model at a time.*
